Community guidelines

NexSpace is your department's home. Keep it useful and safe for everyone.

Do

  • Help each other. Answer questions, share notes and past questions, and credit where things came from.
  • Disagree respectfully. Argue with ideas, not people.
  • Keep posts in the right place — course questions in the course, club talk in its Space.
  • Report anything that breaks these rules instead of replying to it.

Don't

  • Harass, bully, threaten or insult anyone — including lecturers and staff.
  • Post hateful, sexual or violent content, or other people's private information.
  • Share exam questions before an exam, sell answers, or help others cheat on graded work.
  • Spam, advertise repeatedly, or post misleading information, scams or fake opportunities.
  • Upload files you don't have the right to share.
  • Use anonymous posting to attack someone. Anonymous means anonymous to students, not to moderators.

What happens if rules are broken

Content reported by five people is hidden until a moderator reviews it. Moderators can remove content (the author loses 20 NexScore), suspend an account for 7 days (read-only), or — for serious or repeated problems — department admins can ban it. Every action is recorded in a moderation log.
