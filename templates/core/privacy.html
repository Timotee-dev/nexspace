Privacy notice

What NexSpace stores about you, why, and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Your account: name, email, department, level, password (stored as a one-way hash we can't read), and your matric number if you add it.
  • Your profile: photo, bio, skills, links and interests — only what you choose to add.
  • What you do on NexSpace: posts, comments, votes, polls, uploads, ratings, follows, saved posts, Space and study group memberships, and reports you make.
  • Technical data: when you were last active (for department-level counts), and a browser key if you turn on push notifications.
  • NexAI: your questions are not stored. We only count how many you ask, to apply the daily limit.

Who can see it

  • Your posts and profile are visible to people in your department only. You can limit your profile further in Settings → Privacy. Nothing is public on the internet.
  • Posts in 🔒 private Spaces are visible to that Space's approved members only.
  • Anonymous posts show "Anonymous Student" to everyone else. Department moderators can see who wrote one if it's reported for abuse; every such lookup is logged.
  • Department admins can see account details (not passwords) to manage the platform. Analytics show counts only, never individual browsing.

Services we use

NexSpace runs on Render (hosting) and Supabase (database), stores files on Cloudinary, sends email through Brevo, and — if switched on — sends course material excerpts to Anthropic to write NexAI answers. They process data only to provide those services.

Your rights

  • Edit or remove your profile information at any time in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account. Your personal details are erased; your posts stay under "Deleted user" unless you choose to delete them too.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or raise a concern by contacting the NexSpace team or your department admin.
